# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in GTA San Andreas: Ultimate Edition.

## Before contributing

- Keep changes relevant to the project.
- Do not submit copyrighted game files or third-party mod files unless redistribution is explicitly permitted.
- Do not remove or alter mod authors' required credits or notices.
- Link to the original mod page when proposing a new mod.
- Clearly identify whether a suggestion is tested or unverified.

## Suggesting a mod

Please include:

- Mod name and original author.
- Official source page.
- What it changes.
- Dependencies and supported game version.
- Known compatibility or performance concerns.
- License or redistribution terms, if available.

## Reporting bugs

Open an issue with the game version, steps to reproduce, expected behavior, actual behavior, and relevant screenshots or logs. Avoid posting personal information or files you do not have permission to share.

Contributions may be reviewed for compatibility, stability, permissions, and documentation quality before being accepted.
